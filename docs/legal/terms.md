website ("you", "the family admin").

## 1. What Aangan does

1.1 We make and run a private website for your family (your "family site") at an address like
`yourfamily.[AANGAN-DOMAIN]`. We look after the database, sign-in, emails, reminders, backups, security and updates.

1.2 Your family site is private. Only people you or your site's stewards invite, or whose request to join is approved,
can sign in and see anything.

1.3 The software that runs your site is open source (github.com/aangan-family/aangan). You may move your family to a
copy you run yourself at any time; we'll give you your data to do so (section 7).

## 2. Your part

2.1 You'll use the family site for your own family, not for any business or public purpose.

2.2 You'll only add people you're related to, and only details you believe they'd be comfortable sharing within the
family. Each relative who joins controls their own details and gives their own consent on the site.

2.3 You won't add anyone's government ID numbers, bank or card details, passwords, medical records, or anything unlawful.

2.4 You'll keep your sign-in safe and turn on 2-step sign-in. You're responsible for whom you make an admin or steward.

2.5 You'll tell us at [EMAIL] if you think someone got into your site who shouldn't have.

## 3. Your family's data

3.1 It's your family's. We use it only to run your family site and to look after it (keeping it safe, backed up and
working). We never sell it, never show it to anyone outside your family, never use it for advertising, and never use
it to train AI models.

3.2 We don't look at your family's data, except: when you ask us to help with a problem; when needed to keep the
service secure or working; or when the law requires it. Any access is logged.

3.3 How we handle personal data is in our [Privacy notice](privacy.md).

4.3 If a payment fails we'll tell you and try again for 7 days. After that your family site is **paused**: relatives see
a polite notice, nothing is deleted, and you can still download everything.

5.1 We work hard to keep your site running and safe, but we can't promise it will never be unavailable or that nothing
will ever go wrong. We keep nightly encrypted backups and test that they restore.

5.2 If we find that your family's data has been seen by someone who shouldn't have seen it, we'll tell you promptly and
report it as the law requires.

5.3 To the extent the law allows, our total liability to you is limited to the amount you paid us in the 12 months
before the problem. Nothing here limits liability that can't be limited by law.

## 6. Suspending a site

We may pause a family site, after telling you why where we can, if it's used for something other than a family, breaks
the law, puts other families' sites at risk, or if payment has stopped as in 4.3.

7.2 When you cancel, or 60 days after a site is paused for non-payment, we delete your family's database, photos and
backups. Backups are gone within a further 30 days. We'll confirm by email.

7.3 We keep only what the law requires us to keep: invoices and payment records, not your family's data.
