# Aangan: Privacy notice (DRAFT)

*Draft for legal review. Last changed: [DATE]. Version 0.1. Available in English, हिन्दी and தமிழ் [to add].*

This notice explains what **[ENTITY]** ("Aangan", "we") does with personal data when we run private family websites.
It covers two groups of people:
- **Family admins** who sign up and pay for a family site;
- **Relatives** whose details are on a family site we run.

Each family site also has its own privacy page, and every relative gives their own consent when they join.

## What we handle, and why

**About the family admin** (to set up and bill the subscription):
name, email, family name, city and country, the family's traditions, how big the family is, your answers to the
declaration, payment status (our payment provider holds your card or UPI details; we never see them), and your
setup progress.

**About relatives** (only to run the family site, on the family's behalf):
whatever the family puts on its own site: names, relationships, birth years, and the details each person chooses to
share (dates, places, photos, conversations, messages). Each living relative decides who sees each of their details,
and their details are shown to others only while their consent is in place.

**About everyone using a family site** (to keep it working and safe):
sign-in records, the device's notification address if you turn notifications on, and short-lived security logs.

We don't collect government ID numbers, bank details or passwords (sign-in uses Google or email links, never a password).

## What we never do

- Sell personal data, or share it with anyone outside the family, except the providers below who help us run the service.
- Use it for advertising or profiling, or to train AI models.
- Read a family's data, except when the admin asks for help, to keep the service secure or working, or when the law
  requires. Such access is logged.

Each is bound by its own terms to protect the data and use it only to provide its service to us.

## How long we keep it

- A family's data: while the subscription lasts. After cancelling, or 60 days after a site is paused for non-payment,
  it's deleted; backups are gone within a further 30 days.
- Each family site deletes some things sooner on its own (for example, decided requests to join after 90 days, the
  change log after 3 years); its privacy page lists them.
- Billing records: as long as tax law requires.

## Children

A family site never stores phone numbers, emails or addresses of anyone under 18. A child can join only with a parent's
consent, and a parent approves anything a child adds. Children are not shown as "online" and don't use the writing help.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw your consent, or name someone to act for you.
- On a family site: **My privacy** lets you change who sees each detail, withdraw consent, or ask for your details to be
  removed; or ask the family's admin.
- From Aangan: write to [EMAIL]. We'll reply within [X] days.

If you're not satisfied, contact our grievance officer: **[GRIEVANCE OFFICER]**, [EMAIL], [ADDRESS]. You may also
complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## If something goes wrong

If personal data is seen by someone who shouldn't have seen it, we'll tell the family admin and the people affected,
and report it to the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

## Changes

We'll tell family admins by email before any change that matters, and show the date at the top.
