# Code of conduct

Aangan is for every Indian family: any community, religion, caste, language, region or way of life.
Everyone who takes part in this project (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews of family words) agrees to this.

## Be kind and respectful

- Assume good intent. Families do things differently, and many words and customs are right for one family and not another.
  When reviewing family words or festivals, say "in our family we say…", not "that's wrong".
- Welcome newcomers, elders and people who are not technical. Explain plainly.
- Disagree about ideas, never about people.

## Never acceptable

- Insults, harassment, threats or slurs, including about caste, religion, community, gender, sexuality, disability, age or region.
- Claiming one community, caste or religion is better than another, or pushing a single way of doing traditions on everyone.
- Sharing anyone's private details, including **any real family's data**, in issues, screenshots or code.
- Unwanted sexual attention, or content that isn't suitable for a family project.

## If something happens

Report it privately to the maintainers through the repository's [Security tab](https://github.com/aangan-family/aangan/security)
(**Report a vulnerability** — we read conduct reports there too), or by messaging a maintainer on GitHub.
We'll keep it confidential, listen to everyone involved, and may warn, remove content, or ban someone from the project.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
